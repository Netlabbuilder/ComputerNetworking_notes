**Layer 2 Virtual Private Network - L2VPN**:
1. Virtual Private Wire Service (VPWS) is a Point-to-Point (P2P) Layer 2 service transport technology (also known as 
Ethernet Line, or E-Line).
2. (Virtual Private LAN Service (VPLS) is MultiPoint-to-MultiPoint (MP2MP) Layer 2 service transport technology (also known as 
Ethernet Local Area Network, or E-LAN).
